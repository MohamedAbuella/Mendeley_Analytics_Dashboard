Methodology & Automated Analysis Pipeline (Fig. 1 of Abuella, 2024)

This interactive dashboard implements the end-to-end automated academic literature review workflow described in "Automating Academic Document Analysis with ChatGPT: A Mendeley Case" (Mohamed Abuella, 2024). Directly inside your browser, it parses standard Mendeley-exported .bib files, extracts structured bibliometric fields (title, abstract, author, year, journal, mendeley-tags, keywords), computes TF-IDF weighted n-gram vectors, clusters documents into latent research themes, and renders publication-grade figures and tables with high-DPI PNG, vector PDF, and plain-text TXT exports.

Stage 1
BibTeX Ingestion
Parses @article, @techreport, @misc, @book, @inproceedings, braces & LaTeX accents.
Stage 2
NLP Text Cleaning
Tokenizes title + abstract, strips academic stopwords, normalizes domain terms.
Stage 3
Bibliometric Profiling
Computes yearly growth, venue concentration, author productivity, and Mendeley tag frequencies.
Stage 4
Word Clouds & Topics
Generates high-density Title/Abstract word clouds and 6-cluster TF-IDF cosine topic assignments.
Stage 5
Interactive Export
Zoomable canvases & tables with instant ⬇️ PNG, ⬇️ PDF, ⬇️ TXT, and ⬇️ CSV downloads.
